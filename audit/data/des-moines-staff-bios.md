Des Moines Recovery
Wesley Starlin - Executive Director
Wesley Starlin is a licensed mental health counselor with over 15 years of experience leading clinical programs across inpatient, outpatient, and community-based behavioral health settings.  His background blends hands-on clinical work with executive leadership in operations, program development, and team management.  Known for a practical, person-centered approach, Wesley focuses on building strong teams and delivering high-quality care that makes a meaningful impact in the communities and people he serves.
Lacey Stielow, MSN, RN - Director of Nursing
Lacey Stielow, MSN, RN, serves as the Director of Nursing at Des Moines Wellness Center. With a background in emergency and critical care nursing, nursing education, and healthcare leadership, she is passionate about providing compassionate, patient-centered care while supporting and developing strong clinical teams. Lacey is committed to fostering a culture of trust, teamwork, and continuous learning, and believes every patient deserves to feel respected, supported, and empowered on their journey to recovery and wellness. She is especially passionate about mentoring nurses, building collaborative teams, and helping individuals navigate some of life’s most challenging moments with dignity and hope. Her leadership philosophy centers on serving others, promoting excellence, and creating an environment where both patients and staff can thrive.
Parneet “Pam” Sahota, MA, LMHC, IADC, CCMHC - Clinical Director
Parneet “Pam” Sahota, serves as the Clinical Director of Des Moines Wellness Center, where she oversees clinical programming and ensures the delivery of high-quality, evidence-based behavioral healthcare. With extensive experience treating individuals, couples, adolescents, and families, Pam specializes in helping clients navigate co-occurring mental health and substance use disorders through compassionate, trauma-informed care.
Pam earned her Master of Arts in Clinical Mental Health Counseling and is currently pursuing a Ph.D. in Traumatology. As a Licensed Mental Health Counselor (LMHC), International Alcohol and Drug Counselor (IADC), Certified Clinical Mental Health Counselor (CCMHC), and Substance Abuse Professional (SAP), she brings a diverse clinical skill set to her work. She is also EMDR-trained and DBT-certified, integrating evidence-based approaches that promote healing, resilience, and long-term recovery.
Throughout her career, Pam has worked with diverse populations in both mental health and addiction treatment settings, addressing trauma, anxiety, depression, substance use disorders, emotional regulation challenges, and relationship concerns. Her treatment philosophy centers on meeting individuals where they are, creating a supportive and nonjudgmental environment, and empowering clients to achieve meaningful, lasting change.
As Clinical Director, Pam is dedicated to fostering clinical excellence, supporting staff development, implementing best practices, and ensuring treatment remains ethical, individualized, and outcome-driven. She is passionate about helping individuals build resilience, strengthen relationships, overcome obstacles, and create healthier, more fulfilling lives.
Alexander "Alex" Maddux, Director of Operations
Alexander Maddux serves as Director of Operations, bringing more than a decade of experience across behavioral health, rehabilitation, case management, corrections, and security operations. Throughout his career, he has dedicated himself to supporting individuals facing mental health challenges, substance use disorders, and complex life circumstances, helping them access resources, navigate barriers, and build pathways toward lasting stability.
Prior to joining the organization, Alex held leadership roles in community behavioral health, where he coordinated care for individuals with serious mental illness, managed crisis situations, and collaborated with multidisciplinary teams to promote recovery and long-term success. His diverse professional background also includes experience in juvenile corrections, correctional healthcare, and security supervision, providing him with extensive expertise in crisis intervention, de-escalation, risk assessment, investigations, and maintaining safe, structured environments.
Known for his strong leadership, professionalism, and commitment to client-centered care, Alex combines operational excellence with a compassionate, recovery-oriented approach. As Director of Operations, he is focused on fostering a culture of accountability, safety, and high-quality care while supporting both staff and clients in achieving positive outcomes.
Bethany Hamilton, RCS, CMA - Case Manager
Bethany Hamilton serves as a Case Manager at Des Moines Wellness Center, where she is committed to helping individuals build a strong foundation for lasting recovery and long-term wellness.
Bethany works closely with each client to develop personalized discharge and aftercare plans that support continued success beyond treatment. She is passionate about ensuring every individual has access to the resources, guidance, and encouragement they need as they transition into the next stage of their recovery journey.
Known for her compassionate and client-centered approach, Bethany strives to create an environment where every person feels heard, supported, empowered, and hopeful. She is honored to be part of each client's journey and is dedicated to helping them achieve lasting healing and a brighter future.
